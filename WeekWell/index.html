YOUR WEEK, YOUR WAY

Planner preferences

Breaks apply once on Monday–Friday when regular work is planned, to the main customer only. Extra billed assignments are never reduced. You can override the break and main customer using each day's break button. The Friday forecast compares your goal with five equal working days.

Preferences apply to all saved weeks. Daily break overrides stay unchanged.

Daily unpaid break

Deducted only from this customer's regular hours, up to their total. Extra billed assignments and empty days never lose break time.
